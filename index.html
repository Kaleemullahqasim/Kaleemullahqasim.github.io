About me

I work on making language models reason in ways that can be checked, and on making them cheap enough to run where the work is: small models, bounded memory, consumer hardware.

My research covers structured and verifiable reasoning in large and small language models, reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards, decentralised multi-agent coordination, LLM reliability evaluation (hallucination detection and memory faithfulness), and efficient inference (KV cache compression and on-device NPU deployment).

I passed my PhD defence at Southwest Jiaotong University on 30 October 2026, advised by Prof. Jiashu Zhang. I am the technical lead on a research contract commissioned by Huawei on recursive reasoning agents, and I led a research team of three master's students and two PhD students during my doctoral studies.

I have written 24 papers: 14 are published or accepted and 10 are under review or revision. I am first author on 17 of them.

What I am working on

Structured and verifiable reasoning

RDoLT [J3] replaced the single long chain of thought with a verified sub-problem tree that carries knowledge between branches, outperforming chain-of-thought and tree-of-thought prompting on GSM8K, MATH and MMLU-STEM. The programme since then pushes on three fronts: process-level verifiable rewards trained with GRPO [S4, S5], curriculum-scheduled recursion depth [J10, C2], and a destroy-repair search over reasoning chains that improves small models with no additional training [J8].

Efficient inference and the KV cache

A sustained argument that the KV cache is largely redundant. Exact recovery of keys and values from the residual stream permits lossless decoding at bounded memory [R2]; stabilising that stream lets a decoder be trained cache-free from the outset [J9]; and characterising reachable cache states bounds what any residual compression scheme can achieve in hybrid-attention models [S2].

On-device reasoning on consumer NPUs

Published on-device numbers are frequently invalid because the model silently falls back to CPU or GPU. I built a four-signal residency protocol that gates every measurement, applied it to LUT4/6/8 and FP16 builds of Llama-3.2-1B on the Apple Neural Engine, and exposed a precision-independent weight-traffic floor in the decode path that caps achievable speedup [S6].

Decentralised multi-agent coordination

Identical frozen agents that organise themselves through local rules rather than a central orchestrator, with the rules taken from biology and tested honestly, including where the analogy breaks. Task-dependent latent collaboration decides when agents should share state at all [S1]; residual replay makes agent-to-agent communication compact and lossless [J11].

Research software

CGAR: curriculum-guided adaptive recursion

Full training and evaluation code for [J10]. A progressive depth curriculum plus hierarchical supervision weighting cuts training time for tiny recursive models from 10.93 to 6.38 hours on 423,168 Sudoku-Extreme puzzles, a 1.71x speedup for 0.63 points of accuracy.

NL-CGAR: natural-language access to recursive experts

Routes natural-language problems to curriculum-guided recursive experts, with run scripts, datasets and result files.

OpenBanana: editable diagram recovery

Uses fine-tuned SAM3 segmentation and local vision-language models to convert diagrams in images and PDFs into editable DrawIO XML.

Apple Neural Engine inference and profiling harness

CoreML and anemll toolchain for converting, quantising and profiling language models on the ANE, including the four-signal residency protocol used in [S6]. It rejects any measurement that silently falls back to another compute unit.

Reasoning evaluation harness

Reproducible multi-model, multi-benchmark runner across MLX, vLLM and HuggingFace backends (GSM8K, MATH-500, AIME, U-MATH), used for the experiments in [J8], [J10], [S3] and [S4].

Paper repositories

One public repository per manuscript for the KV cache and multi-agent lines: KV-Direct, KV-Free-Transformers, sliding-window-wall, latent-collab-mas and RDC. Full implementations are released paper by paper on acceptance.
